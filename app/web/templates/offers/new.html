Analyze an offer

Paste the offer's HTML fragment (recommended), its plain text, or both. If the description and the application form live on different pages, paste them one after the other in the same box.

Give the offer's link too: it is what lets you find the posting again later. To get the fragment: open the offer, right-click the block you want, Inspect, right-click the element, Copy outerHTML, and paste it below. Content loaded in another frame, or behind another click, has to be copied and pasted too, after the first paste.
