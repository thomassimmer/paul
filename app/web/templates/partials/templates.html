Templates

Your CV and your cover letter should look like you. Import a .docx you are happy with: Paul reads its structure, colours and fonts, and renders your documents in that style. Without a template, a clean single-column default is used.

A PDF template cannot be edited in place: only its look could be approximated, which this version does not attempt. A .doc file has to be saved as .docx first. Text boxes, floating shapes and images of text are only partially understood, and a two-column layout built with a table is re-emitted single-column.
